# Section 10 — Legal / Contracts / Consents Operating Pack

STATUS: DRAFT — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED.
This is an implementation structure, not legal advice and not evidence of legal approval.

## Contracting entity
- legal name and entity type
- registration/CIN where applicable
- registered address
- GSTIN/PAN/TAN where applicable
- settlement account owner
- grievance contact
- data-controller/operator mapping
- provider account ownership

## Customer terms
1. Service scope and marketplace role.
2. Restaurant responsibilities for menu accuracy, preparation, allergens, packaging, licensing and fulfilment.
3. Rider/delivery responsibilities and delivery limitations.
4. Order formation, price components, taxes and fees.
5. Payment authorization, provider confirmation, failure and retry.
6. Cancellation, refunds and chargeback handling.
7. Customer conduct and prohibited misuse.
8. Reviews/feedback integrity.
9. Support and grievance path.
10. Safety and food-safety escalation.
11. Privacy/data processing, retention and processor/vendor sharing.
12. Suspension/termination.
13. Dispute route and governing law as counsel approves.
14. Limitations/indemnities only where legally enforceable.
15. Mandatory consumer rights are not waived.

## Restaurant agreement
- legal identity and FSSAI evidence
- menu/price/tax accuracy
- food preparation, packaging, allergens and labeling
- operating hours and availability
- order acceptance/rejection and preparation SLA
- customer complaints, refunds and food-safety incidents
- platform fees, promotions and settlement ledger
- tax treatment after CA/legal confirmation
- bank/payout details
- insurance/compliance duties where applicable
- audit/evidence obligations
- suspension/termination
- indemnity allocation
- grievance/dispute handling

## Rider agreement
- identity/eligibility requirements
- actual operating relationship/classification as counsel approves
- vehicle/license/insurance requirements where applicable
- safety and incident reporting
- GPS/location collection purpose, consent and retention
- delivery proof and customer-contact boundaries
- payout/earnings/adjustment rules
- cash collection/reconciliation where enabled
- privacy/confidentiality
- suspension/termination and appeal path
- insurance and indemnity allocation
- grievance/dispute process
- no clause is intended to erase statutory rights or duties

## Privacy and consent registry
Capture versioned evidence where legally required for account data, address/location, precise rider GPS, device/session security, payment metadata, support content, analytics, marketing preferences and vendor sharing.
Each record should contain: document_version, purpose, timestamp, actor/entity, channel, status.

## Grievance system
Required categories: payment, refund, food safety, rider/customer safety, privacy/data, account access, restaurant complaint, rider complaint.
AI may triage and draft; accountable operations/legal staff own high-risk resolution.

## Approval gate before pilot
- legal entity mapping approved
- customer terms approved
- partner agreement approved
- rider agreement approved
- privacy notice approved
- grievance route tested
- tax/settlement treatment approved by CA/legal
- FSSAI/e-commerce operating checks approved

Founder-liability note: contracts, indemnity and insurance can allocate operational risk but cannot guarantee zero statutory or regulatory liability.

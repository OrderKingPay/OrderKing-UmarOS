# Legal / compliance operating pack — DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW

This is an operational template, not legal advice and not evidence of regulatory approval.

## Customer-facing pack

Required published documents before public launch:
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Notice
- Refund/Cancellation Policy
- Grievance / customer support process
- Payment/fee disclosures
- Consent and communication preferences
- Location/GPS disclosure where used
- Safety escalation instructions
- Affiliate/partner disclosure where applicable

## Restaurant / partner pack

The actual agreement should define:
- restaurant identity and onboarding verification
- menu/pricing/tax accuracy
- food safety and applicable licence responsibility
- allergens and product information
- preparation time and order acceptance/rejection
- packaging and handoff standards
- customer complaint and refund cooperation
- commission/fees/settlement
- payout disputes
- promotional participation
- data use and access
- account suspension/termination
- indemnity and insurance allocation
- records/audit cooperation

## Rider pack

The actual agreement should define:
- identity and eligibility
- vehicle/document requirements where applicable
- safety obligations
- traffic-law compliance
- GPS/location consent and purpose
- pickup/delivery conduct
- customer privacy
- cash handling if enabled
- incident reporting
- earnings and payout rules
- insurance/accident responsibilities
- suspension/termination
- grievance and escalation

## KingPay / financial features

Do not describe OrderKing as a bank, wallet, TPAP, PSP, PPI issuer, lender or other regulated entity unless the exact legal/partner structure supports the claim.

Consumer UPI/TPAP/payment capabilities stay feature-disabled until the actual sponsor-bank/provider and compliance path is in place.

VIP subscription activation must be tied to verified payment confirmation.

Task/reward programs must disclose:
- task eligibility
- payout basis
- fees
- withholding/tax treatment where applicable
- fraud/abuse rules
- reversal rules
- dispute process

## Data protection

The final privacy/consent architecture must be reviewed against the applicable Indian data-protection framework and the actual data flows used by Customer, Partner, Rider and Umar OS.

## Launch requirement

No document in this directory is “approved” until counsel reviews the exact legal entity, operating model, providers, contracts and jurisdiction-specific obligations.

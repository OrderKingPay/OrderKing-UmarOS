# Section 10 — Legal / Contract / Consent Pack
## DRAFT — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED

This is an implementation draft, not legal advice and not evidence of approval.

### Customer
Final terms must cover account use, ordering, restaurant disclosures, pricing, payments, refunds, cancellation, delivery estimates, privacy, promotions, prohibited misuse, support/grievance and legally valid limitation/indemnity provisions.

### Restaurant
Final agreement must cover lawful operation, applicable food-safety/FSSAI duties, menu/allergen accuracy, preparation/packaging, availability, cancellations/refunds, commercial terms, settlements, taxes, privacy, insurance where appropriate, indemnity, suspension and termination.

### Rider
Final agreement must be aligned with counsel's actual classification. Cover identity/eligibility, license/vehicle/insurance requirements where applicable, safety, location-data consent, dispatch, customer privacy, proof of delivery, incidents, payouts, fraud/GPS manipulation, insurance/indemnity and grievance.

### Privacy / consent
Implement purpose-specific notices, location consent, marketing controls, AI disclosures where applicable, retention/deletion controls, data-request handling, vendor/processor records and incident procedures.

### Refund / grievance
Every case stores case ID, subject, order/payment reference, reason, evidence, decision, amount, actor, timestamp and final state. Payment, safety, identity and legal cases get accountable escalation.

### Acceptance evidence
Store document version/hash, entity ID, acceptance timestamp, actor, acceptance surface and withdrawal/update path.

No document becomes an approved launch artifact until counsel/authorized business owner signs off for the actual operating entity and geography.

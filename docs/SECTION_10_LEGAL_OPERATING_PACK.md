# Section 10 — legal/compliance operating pack

**Status: DRAFT / COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED.** This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice or a substitute for Indian counsel.

## 1. Entity and role map

Confirm the actual contracting entity, registered address, ownership, GST position, bank accounts, payment-provider accounts and who acts as marketplace/e-commerce FBO/operator under the chosen model.

## 2. Customer terms

Must accurately cover: order formation, restaurant role, delivery role, fees, taxes, payment authorization, cancellations, refunds, substitutions, support, prohibited conduct, privacy, grievance and dispute process. Do not promise absolute founder immunity or waive mandatory consumer rights.

## 3. Restaurant agreement

Activation checklist should cover:
- legal/business identity and FSSAI proof;
- menu/pricing/hours accuracy;
- allergens and product claims;
- packaging and food-safety procedures;
- order acceptance/preparation/hand-off;
- cancellation/refund responsibility;
- commissions, settlements and tax treatment;
- audit rights, suspension and termination;
- insurance/indemnity language reviewed by counsel.

FSSAI states that e-commerce FBOs facilitating orders/transactions require licensing and that sellers' valid FSSAI licence/registration must be displayed; FSSAI's December 3, 2024 advisory also emphasizes last-mile food-safety training and truthful online claims. See the official sources listed below.

## 4. Rider operating agreement

Cover actual operating model, identity/eligibility, safety, vehicle/document requirements, payout, delivery proof, conduct, GPS/precise-location consent, incident reporting, insurance and dispute/escalation. Counsel must confirm worker classification and statutory obligations.

## 5. Privacy and consent

Create versioned notices/consents for:
- customer identity/contact data;
- addresses;
- GPS/location data;
- device/security telemetry;
- payment metadata;
- support conversations;
- analytics and personalization;
- marketing preferences;
- AI processing and provider sharing;
- retention/deletion requests.

India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology lists the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 as an official publication. Map the actual implementation obligations and commencement phases with counsel before pilot.

## 6. Consumer protection

Include a dark-pattern review before release. The Department of Consumer Affairs maintains the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023. False urgency, fabricated popularity/scarcity and deceptive interface choices must not be used.

## 7. Grievance

Provide a real contact route, acknowledgement, escalation matrix, owner and response tracking for payment, food safety, rider safety, privacy, refunds, account and fraud complaints.

## 8. Tax/insurance

Obtain CA/legal review for GST/TDS/settlement treatment, insurance coverage, payment/commission accounting and rider/partner payouts. The app must not assume a generic tax treatment.

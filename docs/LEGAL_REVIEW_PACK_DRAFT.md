# OrderKing — Legal / Contracts / Consents Pack
## DRAFT — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED

This document is an implementation draft, not legal advice and not evidence of statutory compliance or approval.

## 1. Customer Terms — required production clauses

OrderKing operates as a food-marketplace/platform service connecting customers with independent restaurant partners and delivery partners where applicable. The final agreement must identify the actual legal entity and registered details before activation.

Required areas:
- account and identity responsibilities
- restaurant/menu/price/availability disclosures
- ordering and order acceptance
- payment authorization/capture/refund
- cancellation and customer-requested changes
- delivery estimates as estimates, not guarantees
- customer address/instruction accuracy
- food/allergen information limitations and restaurant responsibility
- prohibited misuse/fraud/abuse
- promotional terms
- grievance/support channels
- privacy/data processing references
- third-party provider terms where applicable
- suspension/termination
- dispute/governing-law clauses drafted by counsel
- legally valid limitation/indemnity language
- statutory rights preserved

## 2. Restaurant Marketplace Agreement — required clauses

Restaurant partner responsibilities:
- lawful business operation
- applicable food-safety/FSSAI obligations
- menu accuracy
- ingredient/allergen information supplied to the platform where applicable
- food preparation, packaging and hygiene
- operating hours and availability accuracy
- order acceptance/rejection
- preparation-time accuracy
- customer issue/refund cooperation
- taxes/commercial records as applicable
- payout/settlement reconciliation
- platform data/privacy obligations
- insurance requirements where appropriate
- indemnity for partner-controlled acts/omissions to the extent legally valid
- suspension/termination for safety/fraud/non-performance

OrderKing responsibilities should be limited to the platform/services actually provided and should not be drafted as a promise to control restaurant food preparation.

## 3. Rider Agreement — required clauses

The final rider agreement must be aligned with the actual legal classification used by counsel.

Required areas:
- identity/eligibility
- vehicle/license/insurance/document compliance where applicable
- safety rules
- location-data consent and purpose
- availability/dispatch offers
- pickup/delivery conduct
- customer privacy
- cash collection and reconciliation
- proof of delivery
- incident/accident reporting
- prohibited conduct/fraud/GPS manipulation
- payout/settlement
- equipment/platform tools
- confidentiality/data protection
- insurance/indemnity allocation where valid
- suspension/termination
- grievance/support channel

Do not state that a contractual label alone determines statutory worker classification.

## 4. Privacy and Consent

Implement:
- clear purpose-specific notices
- necessary/optional consent separation
- geolocation notice for riders
- customer location permission explanation
- AI processing disclosures where applicable
- marketing communication controls
- retention/deletion rules
- data-subject request process
- processor/vendor register
- security incident process
- versioned privacy-policy acceptance

## 5. Refund / Cancellation / Grievance

Every case must store:
- case ID
- customer/partner/rider reference
- order/payment reference
- reason
- evidence
- decision
- refund/adjustment amount
- reviewer/actor
- timestamp
- final state

Safety, payment, legal and identity cases require accountable escalation.

## 6. Acceptance evidence

Store for every accepted legal artifact:
- document ID/version
- exact document hash/version
- account/entity ID
- acceptance timestamp
- actor
- acceptance surface
- policy version
- withdrawal/update path

## 7. Launch rule

No document in this draft is considered approved until qualified counsel/authorized business owner reviews and accepts the actual version applicable to the operating entity, geography and marketplace model.

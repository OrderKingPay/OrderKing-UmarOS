# 🚀 DIRECT EXIT & SALE PLAYBOOK FOR ORDERKING / KINGPAY

This playbook is designed to take the founder directly to verified buyers who purchase source code, fintech/food delivery apps, and digital assets.

---

## 🎯 STRATEGY 1: SUBMIT TO HIGH-IMPACT PLATFORMS (Takes 10 Minutes Each)

### 1. Acquire.com (Formerly MicroAcquire) — #1 Recommended
* **URL:** [https://acquire.com](https://acquire.com)
* **Target Category:** Pre-revenue / Tech Asset / Software Codebase
* **Target Audience:** Entrepreneurs looking for turnkey software and venture studios.
* **Steps:**
  1. Click **"Sell a startup"**.
  2. Choose **"Assets only / Tech IP"**.
  3. Set Asking Price: **$8,500** (Accept offers down to $5,000 for quick cash).
  4. Paste the copy from [`HDmaster/FOR_SALE_LISTING.md`](./HDmaster/FOR_SALE_LISTING.md).

### 2. Flippa.com — Fast-Paced Direct Auction & Marketplace
* **URL:** [https://flippa.com/sell](https://flippa.com/sell)
* **Target Category:** Apps & Software -> Mobile Apps / SaaS
* **Steps:**
  1. Select **"Sell an App or Source Code"**.
  2. Set **Starting Bid:** $3,000 | **Buy It Now (Direct Purchase):** $9,500.
  3. Flippa assigns an account manager/broker who actively exposes listings to registered buyers.

### 3. Microns.io — Curated Newsletter for Active Buyers
* **URL:** [https://www.microns.io](https://www.microns.io)
* **Audience:** Over 10,000+ indie makers with cash ready for acquisitions under $20k.
* **Steps:**
  1. Submit the project form.
  2. The founder of Microns features it directly in the newsletter.
